Buying guide

How to spec copper sheet with holes

You do not need a novel in the RFQ — you need the right line items. Grade. Temper. Thickness. Sheet size. Hole. Pitch. Orientation. Open area. Blank edge. Finish. Quantity. Miss one and our quote stalls, or you receive metal that will not install, will not braze, or will not match the airflow calc you already signed off.

The one RFQ line that works

Copy this. Replace the numbers. Send it to us at gargindustrialmesh.com. If every field is filled, we can quote without twenty clarification emails.

Copper perforated sheet — ASTM B152 C11000, temper O60 (soft), 1.0 mm × 1000 × 2000 mm, Round 60° Ø3.0 mm / pitch 5.0 mm, OA ~32% (perforated field), blank edge 20 mm all sides, mill finish, qty 50 sheets. Confirm hole ≥ thickness; state burr side / preferred flatness if critical; packing: paper interleaved, wooden crate.
Spec tip

One sentence of end use after the line is worth more than a paragraph of adjectives. Example: “Facade screen, visible both sides, soft temper for slight curve on install.” End use steers flatness, temper, burr side, and whether we push a borderline hole/thickness combo.

Why engineers should specify fully

“Copper perforated sheet, 1 mm, round holes” is not a specification. It is a wish. Full specification protects your design intent after purchasing, after a substitute mill lot, after a third party tries to “match the sample from memory.”

  • Grade is chemistry. C11000, C12200, and OFHC are not interchangeable for conductivity, joining, or process atmosphere. Write the UNS number. See C11000 perforated copper sheet grades.
  • Temper is behaviour. Soft copper wraps and forms. Hard copper holds a flat lid against a gasket. Punching work-hardens the web. Say what you need after perforation, not only mill temper. See copper sheet temper for perforated metal.
  • Thickness is stiffness and punchability. Hole ≥ thickness is our shop-floor rule for clean punching. See perforated copper sheet thickness guide.
  • Hole + pitch + orientation define OA%. Open area drives airflow, strength, and visual density. See hole size, pitch, and open area.
  • Blank edge is the install interface. We customize 0–100 mm; beyond 100 mm on request. See blank edge margins for perforated sheet.
  • Finish and packing survive transit. Fresh copper fingerprints. State lacquer, oil, bare mill, paper interleave, crate.
Don't order without…

Grade (or commercial name + ask us for ASTM equivalent), temper, thickness, finished sheet size, hole shape + size + pitch + orientation, OA% target or “calculate from pattern,” blank edge all around or per side (0–100 mm custom), quantity, finish, and one-sentence end use.
