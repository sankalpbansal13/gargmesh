End use

Copper Sheet Uses - EMI, Grilles, Screens and Filters

Same press on our floor. Different jobs. Tell us the end use in one sentence. It changes grade, temper, thickness, hole size, blank edge, and what “good enough” means on burrs and flatness. We quote this every day for architects, EMC designers, process engineers, and HVAC fabricators.

Thickness bands below are shop guidance, not code law. Confirm against span, load, hole size, and hole ≥ thickness for conventional punching. Detail lives on perforated copper sheet thickness guide.

Architectural copper cladding & decorative screens

Copper sells itself on colour and patina — whether you call it architectural cladding, a decorative copper screen, or a copper panel with holes. Spec-wise you are buying span stiffness, visual density, and a clean margin for the frame. Soft temper for curved wraps. Harder temper for large flat fields that must not oil-can. Round 60° is the usual visual default; square 90° and hex 60° when the designer wants a grid or honeycomb read. Same geometry often doubles as a copper speaker grille when OA% and finish are set for the cabinet.

  • Thickness guidance: ~0.5–1.5 mm common; heavier for long spans or abuse.
  • Prefer clear mm thickness over gauge talk.
  • State pattern direction vs panel long edge — staggered 60° reads differently with the long edge horizontal vs vertical.
  • Patina / lacquer / unprotected — say it. Fingerprints show on fresh copper.
  • Blank edge sized for hem, clamp, or visible reveal. Unequal margins need a sketch.
Spec tip

Ask us for a small sample coupon of the actual hole/pitch/thickness before you approve a full facade quantity. Photos lie. Metal in hand does not.

EMI / RFI shielding & Faraday cage copper sheet

Copper is the conductivity play for EMI / RFI shielding copper sheet and Faraday cage panels with vents. Grade usually C11000 — or C10200 / C10100 when the print demands oxygen-free (do not write “OFHC” alone). Aperture size and thickness interact with frequency — that is an EMC engineer’s calculation, not a brochure claim. We supply the geometry you specify. We do not invent shielding effectiveness numbers.

  • Thickness guidance: ~0.2–1.0 mm often used; thicker for lids that must stay flat against a gasket.
  • Continuous blank land for gaskets matters as much as hole size.
  • Contact surfaces, plating, and joint design often dominate over OA%.
  • Harder temper helps lids stay flat; soft temper helps formed cans — name which.
Don't order without…

Frequency intent and whether the perforated sheet is a shield wall, a vent panel, or a cosmetic cover over a real shield. Those three are different products.

Copper filter screen & strainer sheet

Hole size is the product on a copper filter screen or strainer sheet. Pitch and thickness support it. Burr side can matter for flow and particle hang-up — specify flow direction. Tolerances tighter than general industrial perf cost more and need to be on the RFQ, not discovered at IQC.

  • Thickness guidance: ~0.3–2.0 mm typical; hole size usually leads the decision.
  • Confirm hole ≥ thickness for punch route.
  • State max/min hole if sieving is critical.
  • Alloy vs media (ammonia, acids, food contact) — copper is not stainless. Say the chemistry.

Copper ventilation grille, heat sink & HVAC

Open area drives pressure drop on a copper ventilation grille or heat-dissipation panel. Grade often leans C12200 when brazing or soldering is in the process. Thickness holds the panel in the airstream. Grounding and lightning-related perforated copper follows the same geometry rules — conductivity grade and continuous blank land matter as much as hole size. Calculate OA% on the perforated field; subtract blank edges from free area if your duct opening includes them.

  • Thickness guidance: ~0.5–2.0 mm common depending on duct/panel duty.
  • State whether OA% target is on perforated field or full panel including margins.
  • Name joining method in the same email as the grade. See C11000 perforated copper grades.

Hygiene & antimicrobial — caveats

Smooth cleanability beats exotic patterns. Avoid tiny holes that trap residue if washdown is real. Deburr requirements belong on the PO.

Copper’s antimicrobial reputation is real in some environments and irrelevant in others. It is not a substitute for validated sanitation protocols, food-contact approvals, or the alloy the plant hygiene regime actually allows. Many food plants will redirect you to stainless. We will say so if the duty sounds wrong for copper — that is better than a rejected lot at receiving.

  • Thickness guidance: ~0.8–2.0 mm for washdown durability (job-specific).
  • Confirm finish, edge condition, and whether copper is acceptable for the hygiene regime before you lock the PO.
  • Do not claim “antimicrobial perforated copper” as a certified performance product unless your project has the supporting data and approvals.
Don't order without…

Explicit buyer confirmation that copper is allowed in the hygiene or food-adjacent zone. Geometry alone does not create compliance.

Acoustic — caveats (need tested system data)

Perforated metal alone is not an acoustic system. Absorption usually needs a cavity, porous backing, and a designed open area. Hole pattern affects the result; it does not replace the backing. If an acoustician specified OA% and hole diameter, GARG punches that geometry. If marketing wants “acoustic copper panel” with no backing build-up and no lab data, manage expectations before the order.

  • NRC / absorption claims require tested system data (face + cavity + absorber), not a hole catalogue.
  • Send us the drawing — we match the face geometry. We will not invent acoustic ratings from hole size alone.
  • Thickness for acoustic faces follows the architectural/panel duty — typically the same bands as decorative screens unless the system design says otherwise.
