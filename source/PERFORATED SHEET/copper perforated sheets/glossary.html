Glossary - Copper Sheet with Holes Terminology

Plain definitions for every term used in the GARG INDUSTRIAL MESH buying guide. Use these words on the RFQ so purchasing, engineering, and the press floor mean the same thing.

Geometry & pattern

Term Definition
Hole size (d / w) Opening dimension: diameter for round; side length for square; for hex, state across-flats (AF) or across-points (AP).
Pitch (t / p) Center-to-center distance between adjacent holes along the pitch line used in the pattern definition.
Bridge / bar (c) Metal left between holes. For round/square on that pitch line, typically c = pitch − hole. Too little bridge weakens the sheet and risks tear-out.
Open area (OA% / A₀) Percentage of area that is hole within the perforated field (not including blank edges). Round 60°: OA% = (D² × 90.69) / P². Round 90°: OA% = (D² × 78.54) / P².
Perforated field The zone that actually contains holes, inside the blank margins.
Orientation 60° Staggered pattern: adjacent rows offset so hole centers form ~60° triangles (common “staggered round” / DIN-style Rv thinking).
Orientation 90° Straight / square pitch: hole centers on a rectangular or square grid; rows align.
Round 60° Round holes on a staggered 60° pitch. Default visual and strength-efficient pattern for many screens.
Round 90° Round holes on straight 90° pitch.
Square 90° Square holes on straight rows / square pitch.
Hex 60° Hexagonal holes on staggered 60° pitch. State AF vs AP for size.
Rv Common European/DIN-style shorthand for round holes in staggered (versetzt) arrangement — conceptually aligned with Round 60°.
Rg / Rd Shorthand variants sometimes used for round holes in straight rows (90°). Confirm drawing language; do not assume letter codes without a legend.
Feed direction Direction the sheet travels through the press. Affects how pattern aligns to the long edge and can affect stiffness appearance.
Pattern direction How hole rows run relative to sheet length/width. State it if appearance or structural orientation matters.

Margins & sheet

Term Definition
Blank edge / margin Unperforated strip at the sheet edge(s). Used for clamp, weld, hem, gasket, fasteners. GARG custom range: 0–100 mm; beyond 100 mm on request.
Equal margins Same blank edge all around (or same on opposite sides as stated).
Unequal margins Different blank edges per side. Requires a sketch or drawing.
Full perf to edge (0 mm) No intentional blank margin. Edge holes may be partial / breakout — state if acceptable.
Sheet size Finished width × length of the piece you receive. Say which dimension is long edge.
Thickness Material gauge as a decimal (mm or in). Prefer mm over “gauge” labels that differ by metal family.
Hole ≥ thickness Shop rule of thumb for clean conventional punching: minimum hole opening not smaller than material thickness. Below that is a process discussion.

Material & temper

Term Definition
UNS Unified Numbering System alloy designation (e.g. C11000).
C11000 / ETP Electrolytic tough pitch copper — residual oxygen present. Annealed conductivity class: min 100% IACS (often ~101% IACS).
C12200 / DHP Deoxidized high-phosphorus copper — friendlier for braze/solder. Annealed conductivity ~85% IACS (CDA); confirm mill cert if you need a higher floor.
OF / C10200 (OFHC in trade) Oxygen-free copper. “OFHC” is a trade label — write the UNS. Annealed: min 100% IACS class. Avoids ETP hydrogen-embrittlement mechanism.
OFE / C10100 Oxygen-free electronic — tighter purity. Annealed: min 101% IACS. Spec only when required.
ASTM B152 / B152M Common product standard for copper sheet, strip, plate, and rolled bar. Perforation is secondary manufacturing on that sheet.
Temper Hardness/strength condition from cold work or anneal (soft, 1/4 hard, 1/2 hard, hard, etc.).
O60 / annealed / soft Soft temper — maximum formability; less springback resistance for flat lids.
H00–H04 (etc.) Temper code families used for cold-worked copper (exact scale depends on standard). Prefer plain language + code on the RFQ.
Work hardening Strength/hardness increase from plastic deformation — including punching of the web between holes.
IACS International Annealed Copper Standard — conductivity expressed as % of the annealed copper reference.

Manufacturing & quality

Term Definition
Burr Raised edge left on the exit side of a punched hole. Specify preferred burr side when flow, gasket, or touch matters.
Deburr Secondary operation to remove or reduce burrs. Cost and lead time item — put it on the PO if required.
Flatness How planar the sheet remains after perforation. Critical for lids, gaskets, and large architectural fields.
Oil-canning Visible elastic buckling / waviness in a thin panel under residual stress or thermal/visual load.
Tooling Punches and dies for a given hole size/shape. Special or micro tooling may extend lead time.
Mill finish / as-punched Surface as produced without decorative lacquer; may show fingerprints and process marks.
Interleaving Paper or film between sheets in packing to reduce scratch and stain in transit.
RFQ Request for quotation — the written ask that should contain the full checklist on How to order.
IQC Incoming quality control — where missing tolerances become expensive.

Application terms (use carefully)

Term Definition
EMI / RFI Electromagnetic / radio-frequency interference. Shielding performance depends on system design; hole geometry alone is not an SE rating.
Shielding effectiveness (SE) Measured attenuation of a shielding system. Not inventable from a perforation catalogue.
NRC Noise Reduction Coefficient — acoustic absorption metric for a system, not bare perforated metal.
Pressure drop Flow resistance across a perforated panel; driven heavily by OA%, hole size, thickness, and approach velocity.
Patina Surface oxidation/colour change of copper over time or by chemical treatment.
