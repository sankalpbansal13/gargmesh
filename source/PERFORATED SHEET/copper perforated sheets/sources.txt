Notes on evidence quality
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- copper.org alloy pages are treated as authoritative for composition, %IACS
  physical properties, and fabrication suitability ratings.
- ASTM B152 full text behind paywall; scope/UNS list verified from ASTM store
  abstract plus widely circulated standard text (item 2). Year of issue on PO
  should match the edition the mill certifies to (e.g. -19, -24).
- DIN 24041 full text is paywalled; naming conventions and scope caveats taken
  from SIS abstract and major European perforator documentation. Some commercial
  pages cite â€œDIN EN ISO 24041â€ for quality systems; pattern naming in trade is
  still overwhelmingly DIN 24041 (Rv, Rg, etc.). Do not equate ISO 7805 or sieve
  standards with DIN 24041 pattern codes without checking the cited document.
- Open-area constants 90.69 and 78.54 are industry-standard geometric shortcuts;
  90.7 is a common rounding. Metalex 90.5 is a coarser approximation â€” flag if used.
- Punching â€œ1:1â€ rules are fabricator guidelines, not ASTM/DIN mandatory limits
  for copper specifically.
