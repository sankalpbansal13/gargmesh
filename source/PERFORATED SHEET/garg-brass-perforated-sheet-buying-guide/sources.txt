Primary / near-primary references used for brass guide technical claims
=====================================================================

Alloy designations & compositions (UNS / CDA practice)
- Copper Development Association (copper.org) alloy data sheets for C23000, C26000, C27000, C28000
- Unified Numbering System (UNS) designations for wrought brasses

Product standards
- ASTM B36 / B36M — Brass Plate, Sheet, Strip, and Rolled Bar (scope, temper, chemistry for blank sheet)
- ASTM B601 — Standard Classification for Temper Designations for Copper and Copper Alloys—Wrought and Cast
- ASTM B152 / B152M — Copper Sheet (cited only to distinguish from brass B36; not primary for brass)

Perforation geometry
- Industry open-area formulas for round 60° (≈ D²×90.69/P²) and round 90° (≈ D²×78.54/P²)
- DIN 24041 family pattern naming (Rv / Rg etc.) — geometry naming independent of material

Punching practice
- Perforator industry guideline: hole ≥ thickness; bridge ≥ thickness (starting rule)

Density
- Typical wrought brass density ~8.4–8.5 g/cm³ (alloy-dependent; confirm mill data for weight-critical jobs)

Notes
- Do not invent BIS numbers without a purchased standard edition.
- Mill certificates cover blank sheet; perforation dimensions are drawing/inspection items.
