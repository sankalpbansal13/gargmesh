Buying guide

How to spec brass sheet with holes

You do not need a novel in the RFQ — you need the right line items. Alloy. Temper. Thickness. Sheet size. Hole. Pitch. Orientation. Open area. Blank edge. Finish. Quantity. Miss one and our quote stalls, or you receive a brass panel with holes that will not install, will not match the elevator cabin sample, or will not hit the open area your ventilation grille calc already signed off.

The one RFQ line that works

Copy this. Replace the numbers. Send it to us at gargindustrialmesh.com. If every field is filled, we can quote without twenty clarification emails.

Brass perforated sheet — ASTM B36 C26000 (cartridge brass), temper O60 (annealed), 1.0 mm × 1000 × 2000 mm, Round 60° Ø3.0 mm / pitch 5.0 mm, OA ~32% (perforated field), blank edge 20 mm all sides, mill finish, qty 50 sheets. Confirm hole ≥ thickness; state burr side / preferred flatness if critical; packing: paper interleaved, wooden crate.
Spec tip

One sentence of end use after the line is worth more than a paragraph of adjectives. Example: “Elevator cabin interior panel, visible both sides, half-hard for flatness, lacquer after install.” End use steers temper, blank edge, burr side, and whether yellow brass or red brass is the better colour match.

Why engineers should specify fully

“Brass perforated sheet, 1 mm, round holes” is not a specification. It is a wish. Full specification protects your design intent after purchasing, after a substitute mill lot, after a third party tries to “match the sample from memory.”

  • Alloy is chemistry and colour. C26000 cartridge brass, C27000 yellow brass, C28000 Muntz, and C23000 red brass are not interchangeable for colour, formability, or corrosion behaviour. Write the UNS number. See cartridge brass and yellow brass grades.
  • Temper is behaviour. Annealed brass forms and wraps. Half-hard and hard hold a flat elevator panel or speaker grille face. Punching work-hardens the web. Say what you need after perforation. See brass sheet temper for perforated metal.
  • Thickness is stiffness and punchability. Hole ≥ thickness is our shop-floor rule for clean punching. See perforated brass sheet thickness guide.
  • Hole + pitch + orientation define OA%. Open area drives airflow, strength, and visual density on a decorative brass screen. See hole size, pitch, and open area brass.
  • Blank edge is the install interface. We customize 0–100 mm; beyond 100 mm on request. See blank margin unperforated border brass.
  • Finish and packing survive transit. Fresh brass fingerprints and tarnishes. State lacquer, oil, bare mill, paper interleave, crate.
Don't order without…

Alloy (or commercial name + ask us for ASTM equivalent), temper, thickness, finished sheet size, hole shape + size + pitch + orientation, OA% target or “calculate from pattern,” blank edge all around or per side (0–100 mm custom), quantity, finish, and one-sentence end use.
