What is machhar jali / darwaze wali jali?

Machhar jali (मच्छर जाली) is the North India trade name for woven insect screen used on doors and windows — also called darwaze wali jali (दरवाजे वाली जाली), mosquito mesh, fly screen, or aluminium / SS door mesh. At GARG INDUSTRIAL MESH, Sector 9 Noida, this hub covers aluminium woven door mesh and SS 304 & SS 202 woven mosquito mesh sold as flexible rolls (and cut pieces) in widths 2–6 ft.

SS 304 & SS 202 woven mosquito / door mesh

Stainless woven insect screen — the same machhar / darwaze wali jali family as aluminium, in SS 304 (premium) and SS 202 (value). This is not SS welded mesh and not industrial filtration cloth. Grades are quoted honestly; we do not ship unlabeled “SS” when you asked for 304.

SS 304 mosquito mesh

Higher nickel (~8–10.5%). Prefer for monsoon-facing doors, pets/rodents, humid NCR balconies, longer outdoor life. Common counts: 16×16, 18×16, 18×18, 20×20.

SS 202 mosquito mesh

Economy stainless — manganese replaces much of the nickel. Suitable when cost leads and exposure is milder. Not a silent upgrade to “almost 304.”

Form & widths

Flexible rolls 2–6 ft; wire often ~0.18–0.35 mm by count. Natural or black coated finishes on enquiry. Cut pieces by sq ft for fabricators.

Is this woven machhar jali or welded mesh?

This page is woven mosquito / door mesh (machhar jali / darwaze wali jali) — plain weave flexible rolls for insect screens. It is not SS or MS/GI welded mesh (rigid square fencing grid). For fencing weldmesh see our SS welded and MS/GI welded hubs.

Do you sell finished mosquito net doors or only mesh rolls?

GARG supplies woven aluminium and SS mesh rolls and cut pieces for fabricators and DIY. We do not sell framed hinged, sliding, or pleated mosquito door systems as finished units. Fabricators use our cloth in those frames.

Is 2 ft to 6 ft the door height or the roll width?

It is roll width — how wide the woven roll is across the weave. Available widths: 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4, 4.5, 5 and 6 ft. You cut length from the roll to suit the opening. Door panel height is a separate framing measurement.

Do you stock 6 ft aluminium door mesh?

Yes. Aluminium and SS machhar jali are available in roll widths through 6 ft — including 4.5 ft and 6 ft — not capped at 5 ft. Confirm current stock when you RFQ.

What mesh counts do you offer (14 / 16 / 18)?

Common insect counts are 14×14 (ALU workhorse), 16×16, 18×16 and 18×18. India buyers often say 14 mesh, 16 mesh or 18 mesh. Higher count = finer openings and better small-insect block with slightly less airflow. State count on the RFQ.

SS 304 vs SS 202 mosquito mesh — which should I buy?

SS 304 has higher nickel and better wet/monsoon corrosion behaviour on fine wire — prefer it for NCR balconies, outdoor doors, pets and coastal-adjacent jobs. SS 202 is economy stainless (manganese replaces much of the nickel) for budget / milder exposure. We quote the grade you order; 202 is not a silent substitute for 304.

Is SS 202 the same as SS 201?

No. Both are low-nickel 200-series economy grades and buyers often say 201/202 together. Our economy woven door mesh grade is SS 202; premium is SS 304. Ask for the exact grade in writing on the quotation.

Aluminium vs fiberglass vs SS — which machhar jali is best?

Fiberglass suits budget low-traffic windows. Aluminium is a mid-tier inland door/window cloth with crisp shape. SS 304 (or honest SS 202 for milder duty) suits pets, main doors, monsoon and higher abuse. Mix materials by opening — you rarely need SS on every window.

Will weld mesh or chain link keep mosquitoes out?

No. Welded mesh and chain-link openings are far larger than insect mesh. For flies and mosquitoes use woven machhar jali (aluminium or SS). Chain link is diamond fencing; perforated sheet is punched plate — different products.

Machhar jali aur मच्छरदानी mein kya farak hai?

Machhar jali / मच्छर जाली usually means architectural woven mesh on doors and windows. मच्छरदानी often means a soft bed mosquito net. This page is door/window woven mesh rolls, not bed canopy nets.

What wire diameter / gauge is typical?

ALU insect mesh is commonly about 0.22–0.38 mm (often BWG-style listings). SS insect mesh is commonly about 0.18–0.35 mm depending on mesh count (SWG/mm on catalogues). Always pair gauge talk with mm on the RFQ.

Can I use this mesh for sliding windows and 3-track channels?

Yes. Fabricators cut roll width/length to fit spare tracks and 3-track aluminium or uPVC window channels, hinged door frames, balcony sliders and magnetic DIY screens. Tell us opening type and preferred width.

Does stainless mosquito mesh never rust?

No grade is lifetime rust-proof on outdoor fine wire. SS 304 resists tea-staining better than SS 202 in monsoon and humidity. Coastal salt may need 304 minimum and discussion of 316 if available. Occasional rinse helps all grades.

Is a magnet test proof of SS 304?

No. Cold-worked austenitic wire can show magnetism. Magnet tests are not grade certification for woven mesh. Prefer written grade on the quote; Mn drop testers or MTC help on critical jobs.

Darwaze wali jali ke liye kaunsa material?

Inland main/balcony door without pets: aluminium is often enough. Pets, monsoon splash or longer outdoor life: SS 304. Budget indoor: SS 202 or ALU. Frame type (hinged/sliding/magnetic) is separate from mesh material.

Is this the same as industrial SS wire mesh filtration cloth?

No. This hub is insect screen mesh counts (about 14–20) for doors and windows. Industrial filtration cloth (very fine mesh counts up to hundreds) is a different product family — ask filtration if that is your need.

How do I measure for a mosquito mesh roll?

Measure the clear opening or channel that the cloth must cover, add overlap for beading/spline, then pick the next stocked roll width that covers the larger dimension without wasteful seaming. Order length (or sq ft) from that width. WhatsApp photos of the opening help.
