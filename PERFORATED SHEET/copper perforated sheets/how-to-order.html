Buy Copper Perforated Sheet from GARG India

Send a complete RFQ once. Incomplete RFQs become clarification chains, wrong assumptions, and metal that fails install. Use the checklist below, then copy a sample email and fill the blanks.

Complete RFQ checklist

  • Grade — UNS / ASTM (e.g. C11000, C12200, C10200) or commercial name + ask GARG for ASTM equivalent. See C11000 perforated copper grades.
  • Temper — Soft / annealed (O60), 1/4 hard, 1/2 hard, hard — and whether that is mill temper or required condition after punch. See copper sheet temper codes.
  • Standard — Sheet product standard, typically ASTM B152 / B152M for copper. See ASTM B152 copper sheet standards.
  • Thickness — In millimetres (decimal). Not gauge alone. Confirm hole ≥ thickness for punch route.
  • Sheet size — Finished width × length (state which is the long edge / feed direction if it matters).
  • Hole — Shape + size (Ø for round; side for square; across-flats or across-points for hex — say which).
  • Pitch — Center-to-center. See hole size, pitch, and open area.
  • Orientation — Round 60°, Round 90°, Square 90°, Hex 60°, or custom per drawing.
  • Open area (OA%) — Target % on perforated field, or “calculate from pattern and confirm.” State if airflow calc includes blank edges.
  • Blank edge — 0–100 mm custom (equal all sides or per side). Beyond 100 mm on request. Sketch if unequal. See blank edge margins for perforated sheet.
  • Finish — Mill / as-punched, oiled, lacquered, other. Fingerprint and patina expectations.
  • Quantity — Sheets or pieces. Call out sample / prototype qty separately if needed.
  • Drawings — Attach PDF/DWG for non-standard patterns, unequal margins, cutouts, or pattern orientation vs long edge.
  • Packing — Paper interleaved, plastic wrap, wooden crate, export packing — say what receiving needs.
  • End use (one sentence) — Architecture, EMI vent, filtration, HVAC, etc. Steers flatness, burr side, and process risk.
Don't order without…

Grade, temper, thickness, sheet size, hole + pitch + orientation, blank edge, qty, and end use. Everything else can be confirmed — but those seven missing items stall the quote every time.

One-line RFQ template

Copper perforated sheet — ASTM B152 C11000, temper O60 (soft), 1.0 mm × 1000 × 2000 mm, Round 60° Ø3.0 mm / pitch 5.0 mm, OA ~32% (perforated field), blank edge 20 mm all sides, mill finish, qty 50 sheets. Confirm hole ≥ thickness. Packing: paper interleaved, wooden crate. End use: facade screen, visible both sides.

What happens after you send it

GARG INDUSTRIAL MESH will confirm manufacturability (hole vs thickness, tooling, margins), quote price and lead time, and ask only for what is still missing. Stock patterns move faster. Special tooling, micro holes, or beyond-100 mm blank edges need explicit confirmation. Flatness, burr side, and packing — if they matter on site, they belong in the first email, not the complaint after delivery.
