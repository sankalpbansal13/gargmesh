Glossary - Brass Sheet with Holes Terminology

Plain definitions for every term used in the GARG INDUSTRIAL MESH brass buying guide. Use these words on the RFQ so purchasing, engineering, and the press floor mean the same thing.

Geometry & pattern

TermDefinition
Hole size (d / w)Opening dimension: diameter for round; side for square; for hex, state AF or AP.
Pitch (t / p)Center-to-center distance between adjacent holes.
Bridge / bar (c)Metal between holes; typically c = pitch − hole.
Open area (OA% / A₀)% hole area in the perforated field. Round 60°: OA% = (D² × 90.69) / P². Round 90°: OA% = (D² × 78.54) / P².
Orientation 60° / 90°Staggered vs straight pitch layout.
Rv / RgDIN-style shorthand for round staggered / round straight patterns.
Brass mesh sheet / panel with holesBuyer language for perforated brass sheet or perforated brass plate.

Margins & sheet

TermDefinition
Blank edge / margin / unperforated borderSolid strip at edge(s). GARG custom: 0–100 mm; beyond 100 mm on request.
Hole ≥ thicknessShop rule for clean conventional punching.

Material & temper

TermDefinition
C26000 / cartridge brass~70/30 Cu/Zn brass — default for most perforated decorative sheet.
C27000 / yellow brass~65/35 yellow brass family sheet alloy.
C28000 / Muntz~60/40 brass — architectural / marine accent callouts.
C23000 / red brass~85/15 reddish brass.
ASTM B36 / B36MCommon product standard for brass plate, sheet, strip, and rolled bar.
ASTM B152Copper sheet standard — not the primary brass sheet callout.
Temper (O60 / H01 / H02 / H04)Annealed, 1/4 hard, 1/2 hard, hard conditions.
DezincificationSelective zinc loss in some brasses in aggressive waters — flag wet/marine exposure.

Manufacturing & quality

TermDefinition
Burr / deburrExit-side raised edge; secondary removal if required on PO.
Flatness / oil-canningPlanarity after perforation; waviness in thin high-OA panels.
Mill finish / lacquerAs-produced vs clear protective coating against fingerprints/tarnish.
