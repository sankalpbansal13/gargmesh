Mechanical condition

Soft vs Hard Temper for Brass Sheet with Holes

Alloy is chemistry. Temper is how hard and springy the brass is. Same C26000 annealed will wrap around a radius. Same C26000 hard will hold a flat elevator panel better. Spec both with ASTM codes — or GARG INDUSTRIAL MESH has to guess which animal you wanted.

Temper designations for brass follow ASTM B601 (used with ASTM B36). Trade words annealed / soft, 1/4 hard, 1/2 hard, hard map roughly to O60 / H01 / H02 / H04. Soft words alone are not a complete PO — tensile/hardness windows are alloy- and thickness-specific in B36 tables.

Don't order without…

Blank temper before punching (ASTM code preferred). Whether post-punch flatten/anneal is allowed. Whether the panel will be roll-formed, brake-bent, or lacquered after perforation.

Soft vs half-hard vs hard — punch, form, flatness

ConcernAnnealed (O60)Half-hard (H02)Hard (H04)
Punching Kind to tooling. Brass punches well vs stainless. Normal production territory for most brass perf jobs. Higher punch load. Small holes need prior clarification.
Forming after perf Best for curves and brake returns. Limited forming; gentle radii OK. Poor for tight bends; springback and crack risk at bridges.
Flatness in service Waves on thin / high OA% / long spans — frame well. Practical flat panel choice for elevators and cladding. Best chance of staying planar — until OA% removes stiffness.
Spec tip

For flat elevator / cabin panels: start at H01/H02. For formed lighting or furniture wraps: O60. For stiff speaker grille faces: H02/H04 with realistic OA%.

What punching does to temper

Perforating cold-works local ligaments around every hole. As-delivered perforated temper is not identical to blank temper without agreement. Post-punch anneal/flatten is a separate process decision — confirm on order.

Temper vs punchability

Industry practice: start at minimum hole diameter ≈ material thickness (≥ 1:1) and minimum bridge ≈ thickness. Brass punches more readily than stainless, but 1:1 remains the safe default.

RFQ language that works

Temper: ASTM B601 H02 (1/2 hard) as mill blank before perforation. Finished use: flat elevator panel — no post-punch anneal unless quoted. Forming after punch: none. Burr side: down (away from show face).
